# E-commerce Customer Sales Dashboard (Excel)

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales transactions to understand customer purchasing behavior, product performance, sales trends, delivery efficiency, and customer satisfaction.

The dashboard provides actionable insights through KPI reporting, trend analysis, geographic analysis, and customer segmentation.

---

## Dataset

- 2,400 sales records
- Customer, product, sales, rating, and delivery information

---

## Business Questions Answered

- Where do customers live?
- How do customers prefer to buy?
- Which products are most popular?
- How many products do customers purchase?
- How long does delivery take?
- How satisfied are customers?
- What are the sales trends over the last 13 weeks?

---

## KPI Reporting

- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- Total Revenue
- Average Customer Rating
- Average Delivery Time

---

## Dashboard Features

### Customer Analysis
- Customer Geographic Distribution
- Gender Split Analysis
- Purchase Channel Analysis

### Product Analysis
- Product Popularity
- Quantity Purchased Analysis

### Trend Analysis
- 13-Week Sales Trend
- Order Performance Monitoring

### Customer Experience Analysis
- Delivery Performance
- Customer Rating Analysis

### Interactive Features
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Conditional Formatting
